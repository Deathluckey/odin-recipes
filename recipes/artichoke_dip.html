Artichoke Dip

A tasty dip that friends and family can enjoy and easy to make

Ingredients

  • artichoke hearts in brine
  • 8oz of frozen chopped spinach
  • 8oz of cream cheese
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1/3 cup mayonaise
  • 1/2 cup of grated cheese
  • salt and pepper to taste

Step by Step instructions

  1. Roughly chop the artichokes
  2. Squeeze excess liquid out of spinach, use paper towel or press liquid out
  3. Place cream cheese in a large heatproof bowl. Microwave in 2 x 30 second bursts or until softened. Mix until smooth
  4. Add remaining ingredients. Mix until just combined, (Don't mix to hard or you will smush the artichoke and spinach)
  5. Add salt and pepper to taste and enjoy with chips :)
