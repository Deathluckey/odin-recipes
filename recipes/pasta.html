Chicken Alfredo Pasta

An easy to make dish that can make in a few minutes

Ingredients

  • One Jar of Pasta Sauce
  • Diced Chicken
  • Chopped Onions
  • Mushrooms
  • Pasta (Any type)
  • Seasoning to taste
  • Cheese

Instructions

  1. boil a pot of water
  2. Adding in the pot and occasional stir
  3. Get a separate pan and Start cooking your onions
  4. Cook your mushrooms
  5. Sear your chicken until golden brown
  6. Add all ingredients together and mix with the pasta sauce
  7. Add seasoning and cheese till suited taste
